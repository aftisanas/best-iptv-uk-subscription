## Research Summary — Headline Findings

1. **The realistic competitive set is link-poor.** The position 2 ranking page on "best iptv" UK (`bestipvuk.co.uk/iptv-uk-streamer/`) has just **1 referring domain (Ahrefs verified)** for the ranking URL itself. Position 3 (`bestiptvfinder.com/best-iptv-subscription/`) has **7 referring domains**. Position 9 (`shatao.co.uk/best-iptv-uk/`) has **3 referring domains**. The top 5 on content alone, excluding video and forum results, are reachable.
2. **Page type is the leverage.** The two reachable text rankers in the top 10 (positions 3 and 9) are both classified by Ahrefs as **"/Article/Comparisons"**. Position 2 has no page-type classification but reads as a comparison-style reseller homepage. The single biggest content lever is structuring the page so Ahrefs/Google read it as a comparison article.
3. **The Reddit position-1 result is uncatchable on content.** It carries DR 95 and ranks for the head term. The realistic ceiling on "best iptv" UK is position 2.
4. **Keyword expansion surfaced multiple low-KD UK terms with stronger commercial intent and lower competition than the head term.** `iptv subscription` (Vol 2,000, KD 2), `iptv provider` (Vol 1,000, KD 2), `best iptv in uk` (Vol 900, KD 0), `best iptv uk` (Vol 900, KD 4), `iptv providers uk` (Vol 350, KD 0), `uk iptv subscription` (Vol 300, KD 1), `iptv provider uk` (Vol 300, KD 2), `is iptv legal` (Vol 1,600, KD 16). The cluster should target these alongside "best iptv".
5. **Backlink universe is concentrated in paid-press distribution.** The position 2 ranker's backlink profile (techbullion, barchart, intelligenthq, analyticsinsight, abnewswire, metapress, mirrorreview, etc.) is almost entirely PR-distribution sites. That's not a content win — it's a paid-PR play, and it's a documented path to position 2 in this SERP.

## Task 4 — Competitor Weakness Analysis

Three competitors fetched and read: **bestiptvfinder.com/best-iptv-subscription/** (pos 3, DR 7, 7 RD), **shatao.co.uk/best-iptv-uk/** (pos 9, DR 8, 3 RD), and the SERP context for **bestipvuk.co.uk** (pos 2, DR 18, 1 RD). bestipvuk's page itself timed out on fetch but is well-characterised in the SERP record.

### What top rankers DO WELL

- **Year-stamped titles** ("2026" appears in every reachable text ranker). [bestiptvfinder, shatao, geekvibesnation]
- **Quick-comparison table near the top** — both bestiptvfinder and shatao open with a feature-vs-feature table. [bestiptvfinder uses 4-col table; shatao uses bulleted feature lists per provider]
- **Author byline + "reviewed by" pattern** — bestiptvfinder uses two named authors with avatars (Madison Parker writing, Jake Lawson reviewing). Strong E-E-A-T signal.
- **Methodology disclosure** — bestiptvfinder shows "160+ services tested, 2,400+ hours research, 25+ guides reviewed". This is the page-type signal in plain text.
- **FAQ blocks** — every reachable text ranker has 4-8 FAQ items.
- **Disclaimer copy on legality** — bestiptvfinder publishes a full disclaimer block ("This article is for educational purposes only..."). Removes legal risk while preserving the affiliate posture.

### What top rankers DO POORLY

- **Generic, non-UK content presented as UK content.** bestiptvfinder.com is a US-leaning affiliate page; pricing in USD, no UK-specific channel detail, no GBP. shatao.co.uk mixes GBP and EUR pricing across providers — sloppy.
- **Thin setup / install sections.** shatao.co.uk's install section is a single paragraph + two stock images. No step-by-step Fire Stick walkthrough.
- **No "criteria" or "buyer's framework" section.** Both fetched competitors jump straight to provider lists without explaining how the rankings were derived. This is the easiest gap to fill.
- **No schema for FAQs** observable in either fetched page's metadata (only basic Article schema on shatao via `og:type article`). Rich-result eligibility unclaimed.
- **No 4K-specific discussion beyond a one-line feature claim.** shatao.co.uk's title promises "4K & 8K Providers" but the page body never explains what the difference means for UK households.
- **No refund-window scoring.** Both pages mention refunds in passing but neither builds a refund-window comparison column.
- **Stale or undated update timestamps.** shatao shows "March 13, 2026 → June 1, 2026" but content was not actually updated. bestiptvfinder shows similar pattern.

### Content gaps NOT COVERED at all by top rankers

These are the gaps the cluster fills:

- **The seven-criteria buyer's framework** as a structured section, not a paragraph. None of the top three reachable rankers expose a criteria list as `ItemList` schema.
- **A line-by-line IPTV vs traditional UK pay-TV comparison** with the substituted generic terms (top rankers either trademark-violate by naming "Sky" / "Premier League" — exposing themselves to TM risk — or skip the comparison entirely).
- **A UK-specific legal explanation** that distinguishes the technology, the licence, the end-user position, and the actionable signals to verify a provider. Top rankers oversimplify ("IPTV is legal if licensed").
- **A multi-device matrix** (Fire Stick × Smart TV × Apple TV × Mobile) showing which devices support which IPTV apps and 4K. shatao lists devices as bullets; bestiptvfinder lists them in passing. Neither builds a matrix.
- **Compliance-clean language**. Every reachable competitor uses banned UK broadcaster names. Our cluster — by virtue of compliance constraints — is the only network in this SERP using fully generic substitutions, which is arguably a trust signal once it's read.
- **Trustpilot / refund-confidence signals.** None of the top three publishes a refund policy excerpt or links to one inline. We do (existing footer link + new inline trust signals).

Every cluster page must include:

## Task 6 — Validated Phase 2 Cluster Plan

### Section A — Strategic positioning

The "best iptv" UK SERP rewards content classified as **/Article/Comparisons** — verified live for positions 3 and 9. The only reseller in the top 10 (position 2, `bestipvuk.co.uk`) wins on a thin 1,997-word page with 1 referring domain because the page reads as a comparison-style buyer's guide despite being a reseller homepage. This site already deployed Phase 1 (trademark scrub + buyer's-guide repositioning); Phase 2 closes the gap by adding the structural signals Google needs to classify this homepage as `/Article/Comparisons` and by building a sub-page cluster that ranks for the parent topic ("iptv subscription", Vol 2,000 KD 2) and the trust pillar ("is iptv legal", Vol 1,600 KD 16) while internally linking the homepage on the head term "best iptv". A pure self-promotional page cannot win this SERP. A criteria-driven, methodology-disclosed, FAQ-rich evaluation page can — and the keyword universe shows multiple commercial-intent terms at KD 0-4 the same content can rank for simultaneously.

This site does NOT compete on price ("cheap iptv" KD 0 belongs to cheap-iptv.tv), speed ("fast iptv" lane belongs to fast-iptv.tv), or 4K-as-headline (british-iptv-4k.com / iptv-uk-4k.com). It owns **evaluation**: best, top, recommended, criteria, compare, evaluation, tested, reviewed, guide. Every keyword the cluster targets sits in that lane.

### Section B — Homepage augmentation plan

Unchanged from the prior plan — augmentation A (Evaluation Criteria, 850-1,000 words after StatsBar), augmentation B (Comparison surround copy wrapping COMPARISON_ROWS, 350-450 words), augmentation C (Methodology section between TestimonialsSection and TrustSection, 320-400 words).
